About MedLeaf Coffe Shop

More than coffee - it's a feeling.

Our Story

MedLeaf started in 2022 in Roodepoort with one idea: create a calm, green space where students and creatives can breathe, study, and connect. What started as a small coffee cart is now a community hub known for our signature Moringa latte and our plant-filled workspace.

We believe great coffee should be ethical, affordable, and served with love. Every bean is locally roasted and fair-trade sourced from sustainable farms.

Our Mission

To provide a peaceful, eco-friendly café experience that supports mental wellness, local farmers, and young entrepreneurs.

Why MedLeaf?

Lo-fi music, natural light, indoor plants, free WiFi. Perfect for studying, working, or just chilling.

We use only organic milk, fresh pastries daily, and our baristas are SCA trained.

We host monthly open-mic nights, art exhibitions by local students, and plant-swap Sundays.
