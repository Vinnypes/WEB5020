About MedLeaf

Our Story

Founded in 2023 in Roodepoort, Gauteng, MedLeaf started with a simple idea: great coffee and a place to belong. What began as a small dream has grown into a cozy community space. We wanted to create somewhere students can study, freelancers can work, and families can relax over a good cup of coffee.

Why "MedLeaf"?

The name "MedLeaf" comes from our 2 promises. "Med" for mindful and medicinal - we care about natural, healthy ingredients. "Leaf" for growth and nature. We serve ethically sourced coffee beans, organic teas, and bake fresh pastries every morning.

What Makes Us Different

  • Community First: We host poetry nights, study groups, and support local artists.
  • Quality Ingredients: Our beans are fair-trade and roasted locally every week.
  • Sustainable: We use biodegradable cups and recycle to protect our environment.

Come Visit Us

Whether you need a quiet corner to work or a warm spot to meet friends, MedLeaf is your home away from home. Pull up a chair, grab your favorite drink, and stay a while. We’d love to meet you.
